### RDA observations

- CoinList is the main RDA platform (Algorand, Casper, Mina, Flow, Celo, Solana)
- FOMO frequently defeats the design intent of an RDA (Gnosis sold out in 10 minutes)
- The collapse of SKALE's Dutch auction under front-running bots is a documented mechanism failure
- Algorand's 90% refund policy is an innovation in buyer protection within the RDA framework
- Pure declining-clock RDAs have all but disappeared since 2019; the market moved to LBPs as the alternative declining-price mechanism

### Bonding Curve observations

- Fei Protocol's $1.3B raise and subsequent collapse is the canonical case of scale risk
- Pump.fun shows that bonding curves work at the micro-cap memecoin level
- Sub-types: IBCO (Hegic, BIOPset), continuous token model (Nexus Mutual), social-token curves (Friend.tech, BitClout), launchpad platforms (Pump.fun, Virtuals, SunPump)
- Theoretically the most elegant mechanism, but the large failures (Fei, Friend.tech) show serious problems at scale
- BitClout's one-way no-redemption curve (~$155M of BTC in, nothing out) is the extreme case of a missing exit mechanism

### DAICO observations

**Near-zero adoption is the core finding.** Vitalik proposed the DAICO in January 2018. Despite the backing of the most influential figure in the ecosystem, only 4 projects (variants included) actually implemented it:

1. **Complexity**: the tap mechanism + refund voting require complex smart contracts and sustained governance participation
2. **Misaligned incentives**: teams have no incentive to voluntarily limit their own withdrawals
3. **Voter apathy**: a DAICO depends on continuous governance participation, but most token holders do not vote
4. **Regulatory timing**: the DAICO appeared just as regulators were shutting down ICOs, so its use case disappeared
5. **Simpler alternatives**: vesting schedules, milestone-based releases and DAO treasuries achieve similar goals at lower cost

ICOVO's failed raise ($803K against a $14.3M goal) is itself evidence that the market did not want DAICO infrastructure.

### LBP observations

- The LBP was the dominant "fair launch" mechanism of 2020-2022, with Copper/Fjord Foundry as the platform of choice
- Raises range from $547K (Autonolas) to $139M (GuildFi)
- Perpetual Protocol (September 2020) was the first Balancer LBP
- Weight-decay designs (e.g. 92.5/7.5 → 50/50) effectively suppressed front-running
